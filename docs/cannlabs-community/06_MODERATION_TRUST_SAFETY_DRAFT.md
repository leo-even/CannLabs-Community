# CannLabs Community — Moderation / Trust & Safety Draft

> STATUS: HYPOTHESIS / INTERNAL DRAFT — NOT PRODUCTION-APPROVED
>
> Requires Legal / Privacy / Trust & Safety review before production use.

## Purpose and scope

This bounded internal draft describes a native Discourse moderation operating model for V1. It is implementation/readiness material, not product canon, public Terms, or final Community Rules.

## Native primitives

Use native flags and Review Queue; warning, silence, suspension and content actions; topic controls; UserHistory; post revision history; group history; concise User Notes; and support through `equipe`. Member-to-member PM and Chat remain off.

## Working taxonomy

Harassment/abuse; privacy/doxxing; spam/scam; cannabis transaction facilitation; professional identity integrity; association identity integrity; medical boundary; legal boundary; high-risk deceptive claims; urgent safety cases.

## Enforcement ladder

1. Content action / education
2. Warning
3. Temporary silence
4. Suspension
5. Permanent removal

Severity may skip steps. Identity or leadership review is separate from content enforcement and does not imply automatic revocation.

## Identity review triggers

Potential events may trigger separate admin review of professional verification, association representation or leadership approval: impersonation, false affiliation, compromised account, deceptive claims, or transactional abuse. No automatic revocation is defined here.

## Evidence minimization

Prefer Review Queue records, UserHistory, post revisions and group history. Add a concise factual User Note only when operationally useful. Never use User Notes as a credential archive, document repository or medical-history repository.

## Appeals

Working path: `equipe`. This MUST BE RUNTIME-VALIDATED FOR SILENCED/SUSPENDED USERS IN TASK 24.

## Public rules draft

- Respectful participation
- Protect privacy
- No spam, scams or manipulation
- No cannabis sales, sourcing or transaction facilitation
- Be honest about identity and affiliation
- Keep medical discussion educational/community-based
- Keep legal discussion informational/community-based
- Avoid deceptive or dangerous certainty
- Report concerns and cooperate with staff

This is draft language only; it is not final Terms-of-Service or legal advice.
